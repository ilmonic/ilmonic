That's the greatest purchase, with greatest of sellers and the biggest of possible prices.
> [!Quote] [Surah At-Tawba, 9:111](https://quran.com/9/111)
> {۞ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱشْتَرَىٰ مِنَ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ أَنفُسَهُمْ وَأَمْوَٰلَهُم بِأَنَّ لَهُمُ ٱلْجَنَّةَ...}
>
> {Verily, Allah has purchased of the believers their lives and their properties; for the price that theirs shall be the Paradise...}

[[Marājiʿ/Imām Ibn al-Jawzī|Imām Ibn al-Jawzī]] made a crucial point (within his phrasing) that I haven't given much thought up until this point: 
> [!Quote] [[Marājiʿ/Captured Thoughts|Captured Thoughts]] Page [80](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=W2SIG2RG&page=80)
>
> Exalted be He whose Love (mahabah) to His slaves precedes their love of Him. He praises them for the graces that He has already granted them. He rewards them for what He already provided them. He values their imperfect traits just because He favours them; thus, He parades them for their fasting and declared that the bad smell resulting from their fasting is beloved to Him!
>
> «صيد الخاطر» (ص28):  
> «سبحان من سبقت محبته لأحبابه؛ فمدحهم على ما وهب لهم، واشترى منهم ما أعطاهم، وقدم المتأخر من أوصافهم لموضع إيثارهم، فباهى بهم في صومهم، وأحب خلوف أفواههم»
