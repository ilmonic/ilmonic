Actions have consequences. Cause and effect at play. And one's current state is a manifestation of past accumulated decisions.
Which implies the literal **vitality** of those decisions, for they perpetually determine where you belong in life *(by the will of Allah ofc)*.

> [!note] Page [71](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=454Q8ZJI&page=71)
>
> He who observes the end of a matter at its beginning will reap its benefits and be saved from its evil. Whereas the one who does not observe the consequences [of his actions], as his own desires has con trol over his decisions, will harvest pain from where he sought safety and will endure discomfort from where he sought comfort (rdba).
>
> «صيد الخاطر» (ص25):  
> «من عاين بعين بصيرته تناهي الأمور في بداياتها نال خيرها، ونجا من شرها، ومن لم ير العواقب، غلب عليه الحس، **فعاد عليه بالأسلم ما طلب منه السلامة، وبالنصب *(التعب)* ما رجا منه الراحة.**»

Precisely what "**Delayed-Gratification**" was all about, and those discipline extremist gurus centered their career around.

[[Marājiʿ/Mufakkirūn/Imām Ibn al-Jawzī|Imām Ibn al-Jawzī]] will then point to role of the past in the decision making process:

> [!note] Page [71](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=LXUE7QTX&page=71)
>
> If a person is therefore interested in understanding the outcome, his past should be recalled
>
> «صيد الخاطر» (ص25):  
> «وبيان هذا في المستقبل يتبين بذكر الماضي»

As in the remembrance of the past you establish that no pleasures savored nor effort expanded endures.

> [!note] Page [71](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=6G99T7Z4&page=71)
>
> However, neither the pleasure that you attained from your sin nor the efforts that you exerted for performing the good deed still exist
>
> «صيد الخاطر» (ص25):  
> «وهو أنك لا تخلو أن تكون عصيت الله في عمرك، أو أطعته، فأين لذة معصيتك؟! وأين تعب طاعتك؟!  
> هيهات، رحل كل بما فيه، فليت الذنوب إذا تخلت خلت!»

And yet perpetual establishment of such fact never ceases the human to keep testing it. Establishing **another** fact, [[Mudawwanāt/The Human Non-Compliance Propensity|The Human Non-Compliance Propensity]].

> [!note] Page [72](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=UTMHWADE&page=72)
>
> **Are you not aware that a matter is judged based on its consequences?** You should heed the consequences [of your acdons] in order for you to be safe, and do not incline towards your sinful desires {hawa) so you do not become regretful.
>
> «صيد الخاطر» (ص25):  
> «أتراك ما علمت أن الأمر بعواقبه؟! **فراقب العواقب تسلم، ولا تمل مع هوى الحس فتندم.**»

And it is worth reflecting no pleasure that's wasn't savored left any guilt nor grief.
And that every other marked its ubiquitous regret:

> [!note] Page [72](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=QTX425YR&page=72)
>
> and it is the bitter regret that remains and lasts without anything to negate it
>
> «صيد الخاطر» (ص25):  
> «فبقيت مرارة الأسى **بلا مقاوم**»
