It is a symptom of great sapience to be skeptical of oneself, expanded on in [[Mudawwanāt/The Indispensability Of Self-Scrutiny|The Indispensability Of Self-Scrutiny]] where I've argued the validity or rather the entailment of such prudence in carrying off this life and the after.

> [!Quote] [[Marājiʿ/Captured Thoughts|Captured Thoughts]] Page [76](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=B53SGFNZ&page=76)
>
> Be mindful and do not track each lightening, For a lightening may carry within destructive thunder. Overlook and you shall be relieved from a seduction,  that makes you dress in a garment of humiliation. The trial of a young boy is to follow his whims, And the ambition of his eyes starts such kind of love.
>
