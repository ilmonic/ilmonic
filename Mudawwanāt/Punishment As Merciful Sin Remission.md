[[Marājiʿ/Imām Ibn al-Jawzī|Imām Ibn al-Jawzī]] has his rational spread out:
If god is so merciful why would he burden a servant of his for no **apparent** reason.
> [!Quote] [[Marājiʿ/Captured Thoughts|Captured Thoughts]] Page [83](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=9S2SJRUF&page=83)
>
> I thought about the calamities and great trials— whose severity is unbearable— befalling most of the people! “Free of all defects is He! Allah is indeed the Most Generous, such superior generosity entails easiness and forgiveness. Thus how is that such catastrophes and hardships are still occurring?” I exclaimed.
>
> «صيد الخاطر» (ص29):  
> «خطرت لي فكرة فيما يجري على كثير من العالم من المصائب الشديدة والبلايا العظيمة، التي تتناهى إلى نهاية الصعوبة، فقلت: سبحان الله! إن الله أكرم الأكرمين، والكرم يوجب المسامحة، فما وجه هذه المعاقبة؟!»

And the reason appears to be **spare** him out of his own wrongdoings.
And if one fails to see such. It's is indeed out of his heedlessness *(ghafla)*, denial or pure obliviousness for his own transgressions.

> [!Quote] [[Marājiʿ/Captured Thoughts|Captured Thoughts]] Page [84](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=S2I4XAI6&page=84)
>
> Therefore, when one o f them is punished for a sin he committed, such a person cries out in contempt, ‘Oh God, for what sin have I deserved this punishment?’ And forgets his previous sins for which the earth would shake due to the greatness of such sins.
>
> «صيد الخاطر» (ص29):  
> «فعلمت أن العقوبات -وإن عظمت- دون إجرامهم؛ فإذا وقعت عقوبة لتمحص ذنبا، صاح مستغيثهم: ترى هذا بأي ذنب؟! وينسى ما قد كان مما تتزلزل الأرض لبعضه!»

Elegant and eloquent as always *(prayers to the English only speakers.)*
