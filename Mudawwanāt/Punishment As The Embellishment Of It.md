The most veiled yet volatile of sanctions *(would love to use "Comeuppance" meaning ironic consequence of arrogance/wrongdoings)* is the actual veiling of the sanctioning aspect from the sanctioned and further more volatile once bewitched. *(enticed with the promise of pleasure)*.
> [!Quote] [[Marājiʿ/Captured Thoughts|Captured Thoughts]] Page [77](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=8DTTTSEN&page=77)
>
> The greatest punishment is that he who has been punished is unaware o f the fact that he is being punished. Even worse, is to be pleased of committing an act that is liable to, or deserving of, punishment such as being happy for earning money from unlawful sources, or for being capable of committing sins. Such a person with this condition will not be blessed with an act of obedience (i.e. good deed] to perform.
>
> «صيد الخاطر» (ص27):  
> «أعظم المعاقبة أن لا يحس المعاقب بالعقوبة، وأشد من ذلك أن يقع السرور بما هو عقوبة، كالفرح بالمال الحرام، والتمكن من الذنوب، ومن هذه حاله لا يفوز بطاعة.»

[[Marājiʿ/Imām Ibn al-Jawzī|Imām Ibn al-Jawzī]], mentions three unforeseen examples in *some of*:
1. The Scholars
> [!Quote] [[Marājiʿ/Captured Thoughts|Captured Thoughts]] Page [77](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=68UHV3KZ&page=77)
>
> scholars— among them— become irritated and frustrated whenever their mistakes are being corrected or refuted
>
> «صيد الخاطر» (ص27):  
> «فالعالم منهم يغضب إن رد عليه خطوة»

1. The Preachers
> [!Quote] [[Marājiʿ/Captured Thoughts|Captured Thoughts]] Page [77](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=IEXQUGIN&page=77)
>
> those who preach to people— among  76 Do not I s t Pride in the Sin Take H old o f Yon  them— are actually phony in their sermons
>
> «صيد الخاطر» (ص27):  
> «والواعظ متصنع بوعظه»

1. The Ascetics
> [!Quote] [[Marājiʿ/Captured Thoughts|Captured Thoughts]] Page [78](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=RACS2877&page=78)
>
> the ascetics— among them— are either hypocritical (munafiq') or pretentious (murd).
>
> «صيد الخاطر» (ص27):  
> «والمتزهد منافق أو مراء.»

-> All converge towards portraying a well fabricated persona for "the people", whilst diverging from finding the truth and accumulating the truest of insights.
> [!Quote] [[Marājiʿ/Captured Thoughts|Captured Thoughts]] Page [78](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=ZYEJAY9S&page=78)
>
> The first punishment befalling these [types of] people is that they become delinquent in finding the truth due to their concentration on people [i.e. they are focused on what people say about them and try to employ all means to have people on their side and follow them]
>
> «صيد الخاطر» (ص27):  
> «فأول عقوباتهم: إعراضهم عن الحق شغلا بالخلق»

-> One veiled sanctions is **the deprivation from devout reverence**.
> [!Quote] [[Marājiʿ/Captured Thoughts|Captured Thoughts]] Page [78](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=SZXTFIUI&page=78)
>
> and from amongst the hidden punishments they also suffer from is that they are deprived o f tasting the sweetness (halawa) of invoking Allah in secret and when worshipping Him.
>
> «صيد الخاطر» (ص27):  
> «ومن خفي عقوباتهم: سلب حلاوة المناجاة، ولذة التعبد»

Except for the select few, that we may Insha'Allah be like:
> [!Quote] [[Marājiʿ/Captured Thoughts|Captured Thoughts]] Page [78](zotero://open/library/items/FYSA9UFZ?annotation=374Y5H9Q&page=78)
>
> These punish ments, however, befall not the believing men and women whom He preserves the earth with, whose actions conform to their hearts, if not better, whose inner is similar to their outer, if not better, and whose volition and endeavours are as high as the sky, if not higher. Whenever people recognise them, they deny possessing such quali ties and keep a low profile, and if anyone finds out about any of their heaven-sent miraculous feats, they deny having such blessings. While people are heedless (ghafla) in this life, they are in their solitude [worshipping Allah devoutly]; loved by the earth and rejoiced by the planets of heavens.  1 ask Allah, Most High, to grant us success to follow their steps and to make us amongst their followers.
>
> «صيد الخاطر» (ص27):  
> «إلا رجال مؤمنون، ونساء مؤمنات، يحفظ الله بهم الأرض، بواطنهم كظواهرهم؛ بل أجلى، وسرائرهم كعلانيتهم؛ بل أحلى، وهممهم عند الثريا1؛ بل أعلى، إن عرفوا تنكروا، وإن رئيت لهم كرامة أنكروا، فالناس في غلاتهم، وهم في قطع فلاتهم2، تحبهم بقاع الأرض، وتفرح بهم.  
> أملاك السماء، نسأل الله عز وجل التوفيق لاتباعهم، وأن يجعلنا من أتباعهم.»
