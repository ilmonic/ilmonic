It's so interesting reading through the thoughts of Ibn Al-Jawzi post reading the almost "glorification" by many of the witnesses of his sermons of his sermons, I find myself way closer to his person then I am to any other person I've met.
It's like ideas and ways of life are replicated by similar souls, experienced the same way.
I've only spent some time reading the short extremely insightful chapters whilst on commute or skipping school sessions and I can't help but highlight all of the chapter from start to finish on my phone, due to how much I resonate with the words and the simple yet captivating logic at display.
Can't help by write down on my school notebooks some aphoristic phrases I wish I could put on my walls, phrases that distill many journal entries and contemplation I've done many times in the most elegant of diction.
And all of a sudden I find myself the one to be "glorifying". That's funny.
